Games on this table are for entertainment. Points, chips or virtual money in games have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged.

Each game is logged: the game settings, the display name you chose, your seat and every move. Logs are kept for a limited time (90 days by default) on the table and on the Game Table service, and may be used to improve games and their AI players.

No login is needed. Use any display name; avoid entering personal information as your name.
