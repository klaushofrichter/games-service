# Terms of service

## Playing

Games on this table are for entertainment. Points, chips or virtual money in games have no real-world value and cannot be bought, won, paid out or exchanged. Nothing here is gambling for money.

No login is needed. Use any display name; avoid entering personal information as your name. Some players at the table may be AI players, and the table shows them as such.

## What is recorded

Each game is logged: the game settings, the display name you chose, your seat and every move. Logs and saved games are kept for a limited time (90 days by default) on the table and on the Game Table service, and may be used to improve games and their AI players. Remote play connects your browser to the table through the Game Table service.
