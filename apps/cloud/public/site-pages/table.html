Tools

Ordinary woodworking tools. Measure twice: the opening for the screen is the one cut that can't be undone.

  • Tape measure, steel ruler and square, and a pencil, to mark the opening
  • Drill with wood bits, to start the cut in the corners and to screw the supports
  • Jigsaw with a fine wood blade, to cut the opening (or a router with a template, for the cleanest edge)
  • Hole saws in the sizes of the power outlet and the volume knob
  • Clamps, to hold a straight edge or the supports while you work
  • Hacksaw or mitre saw with a blade for aluminium, and a mitre box, for the frame's corners
  • File and sandpaper, for the cut edges of wood and metal
  • Screwdrivers
  • Masking tape, to mark on and to keep the top from splintering along the cut
  • Safety glasses and hearing protection

Step by step Draft

The steps in outline. Measurements, photos of each step and what we learned on the way are still to come.

  1. Choose the screen, then plan around it

    Measure the screen's front, its depth, and where its cables and buttons are. Decide where it sits in the top, how far its front stands above the wood (flush is nicest), and where the computer, the outlet and the knob go.

  2. Mark the opening

    Tape the top, then draw the opening a little larger than the screen's front so that it drops in. Check the corners with the square, and measure again.

  3. Cut the opening

    Drill a hole inside each corner, then cut from hole to hole along a clamped straight edge. File and sand the edges.

  4. Fit the supports

    Screw strips or brackets under the opening so that the screen rests at the right height and can't drop through. Leave room for its cables and for air to reach its back.

  5. Frame the opening

    Cut the aluminium trim with mitred corners and fit it around the opening: it covers the wood's cut edge and gives the screen a clean border.

  6. Outlet and volume knob

    Drill the holes in the table's side with the hole saws and fit the power outlet and the volume knob.

  7. The computer and the cables

    Fix the small computer under the top, connect the screen (video and touch), the knob and the power, and clip the cables along the frame, away from knees and feet.

  8. Put the screen in and test

    Lay the screen in, start the computer, and check that a touch lands where the finger is, in every corner.
