What is recorded

Games are logged to replay and improve them: the game, its settings, the display names players chose, their seats and their moves. Logs are kept for a limited time (90 days by default) and may be used to improve games and their AI players. No login is needed to play. Admins of the service sign in with Google, and a cookie keeps them signed in; another cookie lets a screen keep showing a table's board. There are no ads and no tracking across sites.
