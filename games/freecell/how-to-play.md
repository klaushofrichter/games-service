# FreeCell

Sort all 52 cards onto the four foundations. A solitaire for 1 player, played on the board.

## In short

- Move every card to the **foundations**: one pile per suit, from ace up to king.
- In the columns, a card goes on a card **one higher and of the other colour**.
- The four **free cells** each hold one card for a while.
- Every deal can be solved.

## What you see

- **The board** is where you play: four free cells top left, four foundations top right, eight columns below. All cards lie face up.
- **Your phone** says that the game is on the board, and lets you choose how the cards look: six designs, each shown with a few of its cards. Tap one and the board changes at once, also in the middle of a game. The table remembers it.

## Moving cards

- **Drag** a card to a column, a free cell or a foundation. The places it may go light up.
- **Tap** a card and it goes where it fits best: a foundation first, then a column, then a free cell.
- A **run** of cards in order (one lower, colours alternating) moves in one go: drag or tap its highest card. How many cards can move at once depends on the room you have: (empty free cells + 1), doubled for every empty column.
- Any card, or a run, may go to an **empty column**.
- **House rule, on by default:** a foundation's top card may be dragged back to a column or a free cell. Switch it off in the board's settings (the gear) for the strict game, where cards on the foundations stay there. The table remembers your choice.

Cards that are not needed in the columns any more go to the foundations by themselves. Once every column is in order, the game finishes itself.

## The buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Undo** | Takes your last move back, as often as you like. |
| **Hint** | Shows a move that leads to a solved game. If there is no way from where you are, it says so: take some moves back. |
| **Start again** | The same deal from the beginning. Tap twice. |
| **Give up** | Ends the game. Tap twice. |

## The end

The game ends when all 52 cards are on the foundations, or when you give up. **Awards and Results** shows your moves, time, undos and hints, and what you earned: solving without a hint or an undo, within three or five minutes, in few moves, and more.

Each deal has a number, shown on the board. To play a deal again, open the board's settings (the gear) during a game and tap **Deal number**: type the number and confirm. The game that is on ends without a result.
